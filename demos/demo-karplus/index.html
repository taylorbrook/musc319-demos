Karplus-Strong: pluck a string from a burst of noise

Inject a short burst of noise into a delay line whose length sets the pitch, close the loop with feedback, and darken each pass with an in-loop low-pass filter. The result rings like a plucked string and fades as the high harmonics die first. Press Start, then press Pluck and move the sliders.

noise burst → sum → delay (pitch) → low-pass (damping) → feedback → back to sum (and out)

Step 3: one model, two materials

The presets set the feedback and damping pair for you: Bright steel keeps a high cutoff and near-one feedback (a long, ringing steel string); Muted nylon lowers both (a short, dark nylon pluck). Same algorithm, two instruments.

The pluck decay (live scope)

Watch the loop fill on the pluck and then decay: higher feedback holds the ring longer, and lower damping (a lower cutoff) darkens the tone and shortens it, because the low-pass removes a little high-frequency energy on every pass around the loop.
