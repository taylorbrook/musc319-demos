Cello-pizzicato sampler: shape and repitch a real sample

A sampler wraps a real recording, here a cello pizzicato (root note A1), in an ADSR envelope: attack in, decay down to a sustain level held while you hold the note, then release out when you let go. The transpose slider repitches the sample by changing playback speed. Hold the Play-note button and watch the enveloped sample draw itself. Press Start, then play a note.

Live output (time domain)

Attack and release set the in/out times; decay falls to the sustain level, which holds while you hold the note. Short attack + short release is percussive; long attack + long release is a slow swell.
