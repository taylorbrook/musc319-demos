Chip arpeggio: one channel that implies a whole chord

The SID trick from the session, live: one chiptune oscillator steps through a chord's notes fast enough that they fuse into an implied harmony, so a single channel does the work of three. Pick a chip waveform (square, narrow pulse, triangle, sawtooth), pick a chord, set the arp rate, then A/B the one-channel arpeggio against a real held chord played by three oscillators at once. Toggle the noise voice for the PSG-style percussion pulse.
