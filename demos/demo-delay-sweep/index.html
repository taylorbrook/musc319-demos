Delay sweep: one control, three outcomes

Sweep the single delay-time slider and listen to one delay control move through the three outcomes the page names: stereo width at the shortest times, comb coloration and flanging a little longer, then a discrete echo once the repeat separates. The scope shows the comb notches sliding with the delay time; raise feedback to regenerate repeats. Press Start, then move the sliders. The default source is a plucky sawtooth melody (C-D-E-F-G, then rests) whose note attacks let you hear each echo; switch the source to broadband noise for the cleanest view of the comb notches.

Step 3: feedback and mix

With the mix near the middle the dry and wet copies are equal, so the comb notches are deepest. Feedback routes the delay output back into the delay input to add successive repeats, from a single slap up toward a runaway wash as it approaches its top.

The comb notches (live FFT scope)

A shorter delay spaces the comb notches more widely; a longer delay packs them closer. Sweeping the delay time slides the whole comb, which is exactly the swooshing motion of a flanger. Switch the source to broadband noise for the cleanest read of the notches; the melody's harmonics show the same comb carved onto discrete pitch lines.
