Aliasing as a falling tone

A tone is swept steadily upward. Sampled too sparsely, frequencies above the Nyquist limit fold back down: the true tone keeps rising, but the pitch you hear turns at the limit and falls. Press Start, then Sweep. Switch to Band-limited to hear the same sweep rise cleanly with no fold.

Step 2: how the tone is sampled

Simulated sampling rate fs = 8000 Hz · Nyquist limit fs ÷ 2 = 4000 Hz. The sweep runs the true frequency from 140 Hz up past the limit to 7860 Hz.

The foldover map (heard frequency vs true frequency)

Horizontal axis: the true input frequency. Vertical axis: the frequency you actually hear. Below Nyquist the two match (the diagonal). Above it the heard frequency reflects off the limit, then off zero, and so on — one rising input maps onto a pitch that folds up and down. The dot follows the sweep as it plays.
