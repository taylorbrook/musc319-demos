Polar patterns: omni to cardioid to figure-8, and the proximity effect

Morph the microphone's polar pattern with the pattern slider, using the page's own equation r(theta) = A + (1 - A)cos(theta): A = 1 is omni, A = 0.5 is cardioid, A = 0 is figure-8. Then drag the source dot to orbit it around the mic (or use the angle and distance sliders): the level rises on-axis and drops toward the pattern null. Move the source close on a directional pattern to hear the proximity bass lift. Press Start, then Play tone.

Step 1: enable sound

Audio is off.

Step 2: the pattern and the source

Drag the source dot, or use the sliders below.

Omni (A = 1) has no proximity effect. On directional patterns the bass lift grows as the source moves close.

The gain the mic applies to the source is the magnitude of r(theta) at the source angle: full on-axis, zero at the pattern's null. Figure-8 has two side nulls and a rear lobe of opposite polarity (drawn in the warm color); cardioid has one null directly behind. The proximity low-shelf boost is applied only for directional patterns (A less than 1), the way a real pressure-gradient capsule behaves and a pure omni does not.
